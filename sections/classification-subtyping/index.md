title: Taxonomic Classification and Genomic Subtyping
---
Before any genomic comparison can be made, we need to know *what* we are looking at. Species identification is the first level of classification we perform with genomic data, and the answer shapes every analysis that follows — which reference to use, which typing scheme applies, and which samples belong together.

This course covers three ideas in order:

1. **What a species is** — and why species definitions are not as consistent as they might seem.
2. **How we identify species from genomes** — from 16S rRNA to k-mer-based methods.
3. **How we subtype below the species level** — consistent, more granular labels that help us communicate and group samples before genome-wide analysis.
