{%- comment -%} Builds the ordered list of every lecture and activity in the course. Sections come from _data/course.yml (in that order); items within a section are pages whose front matter has `section: `, sorted by `order`. After including this file you can use: course_items – array of all lecture/activity pages, in course order {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign course_items = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for s in site.data.course.sections -%} {%- assign s_items = site.pages | where: "section", s.id | where_exp: "p", "p.layout == 'lecture' or p.layout == 'activity'" | sort: "order", "last" -%} {%- for it in s_items -%} {%- assign course_items = course_items | push: it -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%}
